About MapIT Design (MID)

Professional solar design and engineering support — from proposal to PTO — helping installers scale with permit-ready documentation.

Reliable. Compliant. Fast.

MapIT Design (MID) partners with solar installers, EPCs, and renewable contractors across the United States and Canada. Our services include Aurora Solar proposals, AHJ-compliant permit plan sets, single-line and three-line electrical diagrams, structural attachment plans, PE stamping coordination, utility interconnection packages, and PTO support.

Typical turnaround: 24–72 hours for standard residential projects. Scalable support available for larger portfolios and commercial projects.

Our Services

End-to-end solar project support — clear deliverables that help you submit complete permit packages and achieve faster approvals.

Aurora Proposal Design

Optimized system layouts, production analysis, and customer-ready proposals to increase sales conversion.

Solar Permit Plan Sets

Complete AHJ-compliant permit packages: site plans, roof layouts, single-line diagrams, three-line diagrams, equipment specs, and code notes.

Structural & PE Stamping Support

Coordinate professional engineer review and stamping where required by local jurisdictions.

Permit Submission & Revision Support

Assistance with permit comments, revisions, and AHJ interactions to reduce approval cycles.

Utility Interconnection

Prepare interconnection applications, technical documentation, and single-line diagrams for utilities.

PTO & Final Documentation

Support through Permission to Operate (PTO) and final documentation handoff to the owner/operator.
