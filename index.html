Residential Solar Permit Design

We prepare complete, permit-ready residential PV plan sets tailored to the project location, utility requirements, and local AHJ standards.

Scope Includes

  • Roof and site layout with accurate module placement
  • PV array configuration, setbacks, and roof zones
  • Module, inverter, optimizer and racking information
  • Electrical single-line diagram (SLD)
  • Conductor, OCPD and equipment calculations
  • Equipment grounding and disconnect details
  • Rapid shutdown and labeling requirements
  • Structural attachment details and roof information
  • NEC and applicable local code notes
  • AHJ-specific plan requirements
  • Permit-ready PDF drawing package
